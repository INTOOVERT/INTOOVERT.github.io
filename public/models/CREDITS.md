# Third-party 3D asset attribution

The retro computer model and its baked textures used on the landing page come from:

**Retro Computer Personal Website** by **Edward Hinrichsen** — https://edh.dev
Source: https://github.com/edhinrichsen/retro-computer-website
License: MIT

Files used in this project:

- `public/models/Commodore710.glb` (originally `Commodore710_33.5.glb`)
- `public/textures/bake.jpg` (originally `bake-quality-5.jpg`)
- `public/textures/bake_floor.jpg` (originally `bake_floor-quality-3.jpg`)

Only the on-screen content was changed (the screen now shows an interactive
terminal over a hand-drawn portrait, rendered through a custom CRT shader).
The model and bakes are used unmodified under the terms of the MIT License
reproduced below.

## Other assets

- `public/models/speaker.glb` — desk speaker model supplied by the site owner
  (sourced separately; flanks the monitor in the hero scene).
- `public/media/ambient.mp3` — "Dreamy Flashback" by Kevin MacLeod
  (https://incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
